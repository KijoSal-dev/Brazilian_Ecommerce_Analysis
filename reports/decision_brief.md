# Brazilian E-Commerce: Customer Purchasing, Spending and Delivery Performance

## Decision Brief

**Objective**

Analyse customer purchasing behaviour, spending patterns, payment preferences, product category performance and the relationship between delivery performance and customer satisfaction.

### Key Findings

**1. Customer purchasing behaviour**

The dataset contains 96,096 unique customers. Of these, 3.12% made repeat purchases, while 96.88% placed only one order. This indicates that repeat purchasing was relatively uncommon within the observed dataset.

**2. Customer spending**

Total product sales amounted to **R$13.59 million**, with freight charges contributing another **R$2.25 million**. The combined order value was approximately **R$15.84 million**. Average order value was R$160.58, compared with a median of R$105.29, suggesting that higher-value orders influenced the average.

**3. Payment preferences**

Credit cards accounted for **78.34% of recorded payment value**, making them the dominant payment method. Boleto accounted for 17.92%, while vouchers and debit cards contributed smaller shares.

**4. Product category performance**

Health & Beauty generated the highest product revenue at **R$1.26 million (9.26%)**, followed by Watches & Gifts at R$1.21 million (8.87%). Bed, Bath & Table recorded the highest number of order-item records, but ranked third in revenue. This demonstrates that sales volume and revenue do not necessarily move together.

**5. Delivery performance and customer satisfaction**

Of 95,824 delivered orders with complete delivery dates and customer reviews, 92.01% arrived on time and 7.99% arrived late. On-time deliveries recorded an average review score of **4.29/5**, compared with **2.57/5** for late deliveries. This 1.72-point difference indicates a strong association between delivery delays and lower customer satisfaction.

### Recommended Business Actions

1. **Investigate delivery delays:** Identify the sellers, locations, logistics stages and other operational factors contributing to late deliveries.
2. **Improve customer retention:** Investigate why repeat purchasing is relatively uncommon and consider targeted customer engagement and retention initiatives.
3. **Prioritise high-performing categories:** Review stock availability and marketing opportunities in Health & Beauty and Watches & Gifts while monitoring category profitability.
4. **Maintain convenient payment options:** Continue supporting credit card payments while monitoring the needs of customers using other payment methods.
5. **Monitor delivery and customer satisfaction together:** Track late-delivery rates and review scores regularly to identify operational problems early.

### Limitations and Assumptions

- The analysis uses historical e-commerce records and does not establish current business performance.
- Spending calculations cover 98,666 orders with available order-item records; orders without item records were excluded.
- Repeat purchasing is measured across the available dataset, without adjusting for customer tenure or purchase opportunities.
- Two untranslated product categories were assigned analyst-provided English labels. Records without category information were retained as Unknown Category.
- Multiple review scores for an order were averaged before comparing delivery performance.
- The delivery and review findings indicate association, not causation. Other factors may also influence customer ratings.

**Overall Conclusion**

The analysis highlights strong product sales and a high proportion of on-time deliveries, but also identifies opportunities to improve customer retention and delivery performance. Reducing delivery delays and understanding repeat-purchase behaviour should be important areas for further business investigation.
